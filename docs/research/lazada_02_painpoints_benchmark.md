## 3. LAZADA PAIN POINTS (SELLER + BUYER PERSPECTIVE)

### 3.1 Seller Complaints

#### Commission & Fee Structure

| Fee Category | Typical Range | Seller Complaints |
|-------------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Commission rate** | 2-10% (varies by category) | High compared to Shopee for certain categories; minimum guarantee fees for LazMall sellers |
| **Payment processing** | ~2% per transaction | Deducted automatically, opaque to sellers |
| **Shipping subsidy** | Variable | Sellers must partially subsidize "free shipping" promotions; Lazada subsidizes ~50% but sellers bear rest |
| **Advertising (Lazada Sponsored Solutions)** | CPM/CPC model | Algorithm changes make ROI unpredictable; escalating spend required for visibility |
| **Return handling fees** | Per-return charge | Sellers penalized for returns even when buyer-caused |
| **Promotional sharing** | 10-50% discount sharing | Lazada frequently requires sellers to absorb large discounts during campaigns (7.7, 11.11, 12.12) |
| **Early payment / delayed settlement** | 7-15 days standard | Some sellers report delays up to 21-30 days; liquidity pressure on SMEs |

#### Operational Pain Points for Sellers

1. **Algorithm volatility:** Lazada's search ranking algorithm changes frequently with little notice, causing sudden traffic drops for established sellers
2. **Return abuse ("mua trả"):** High rate of COD returns and buyer-side abuse — buyers order multiple sizes/colors and return most
3. **Poor seller support response times:** Average resolution time for seller disputes: 3-7 business days (vs. Shopee's <48 hours in many cases)
4. **Mandatory participation in mega-campaigns:** Sellers feel pressured into selling at losses during platform-driven mega sales events
5. **LazMall strict requirements:** Brand authentication process can take weeks; penalties for late shipping are aggressive (automatic cancellation + score penalty)
6. **Review manipulation:** Sellers report competing with fake reviews from rival sellers and bot-driven reviews

### 3.2 Buyer Complaints

| Complaint Category | Frequency | Severity |
|-------------------|-----------|----------|
| **Slow delivery vs. promised** | High | LEX (Lazada Express) average: 3-5 days standard vs. promised 2-3 days |
| **Wrong/damaged items** | Medium-High | Especially in electronics and fashion |
| **Refund processing delays** | High | Average 7-14 business days (vs. Shopee's 3-7 days in best cases) |
| **Counterfeit products** | Medium | Despite LazMall, fake goods persist; Philippines IP Office recorded Lazada as **#1 source of counterfeit reports (69%)** in H1 2023 — [Wikipedia: Lazada Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lazada_Group) |
| **Poor customer service** | High | Chatbot-first approach frustrates users; long wait times for human agents |
| **App experience** | Medium | Bugs, crashes, slow loading reported more frequently than Shopee app |
| **Voucher/promotion confusion** | Medium | Complex voucher stacking rules; promised discounts not applied |

### 3.3 Internal / Operational Issues

| Issue | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **COD failure rate** | Estimated 15-20% for Lazada (vs. Shopee ~10-12%); COD refusal is a major cost driver |
| **Return rate** | Overall ~8-12% on Lazada; fashion/apparel up to 20-25% |
| **Seller churn** | High — many mid-size sellers migrated to Shopee and TikTok Shop 2023-2024 |
| **Headcount reduction** | Multiple rounds of layoffs: Oct 2023, Jan 2024 (reduction of 25-50% of SEA headcount reported) — [Wikipedia: Lazada Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lazada_Group) |
| **Cost per order** | Higher logistics cost than Shopee due to LEX fleet underutilization as order volume declined |
| **Marketplace trust deficit** | Brand perception gap vs. Shopee (seen as "less value") and vs. Tiki (seen as "less authentic") |

### 8.2 Lazada's Strategic Crossroads

Lazada faces a fundamental challenge: **massive capital from Alibaba (~$5.2B) has not translated into market leadership or even sustained market position.** Key structural issues:

1. **The "middle child" problem** — Not as large as Shopee, not as trusted as Tiki, not as innovative as TikTok Shop
2. **Technology gap** — Despite Alibaba's tech prowess, Lazada has been slower to deploy cutting-edge AI features in Vietnam
3. **Organizational instability** — 5 rounds of layoffs since 2023 signals internal uncertainty and strategy churn
4. **Seller ecosystem erosion** — As GMV declines, logistics costs per order rise, creating a negative spiral
